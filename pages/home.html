Department of Demography | University of California, Berkeley

I am a first-year PhD student in Demography at UC Berkeley. Taking a life course perspective, I study how social context shapes family formation and dissolution and how the family unit in turn shapes outcomes for the next generation. My current research focuses on immigrant families to understand how families form under pressure, what holds them together, and what gets carried forward.

My path to demography has been unconventional, bringing a technical and data-focused approach to my work. I studied Mathematics and Computer Science at MIT and worked as a software engineer at Capital One before pivoting to population research. I hold a Master's in Data Science from the University of Helsinki and a European Master's in Demography from the European Doctoral School of Demography (EDSD), where I trained at the Max Planck Institute for Demographic Research in Rostock and INED in Paris.
